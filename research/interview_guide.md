# Future user interview guide

**Status: planned guide only. No interviews have been conducted.**

Purpose: learn how people discover devotional content and evaluate digital puja or chadhava journeys. Participation must be voluntary; do not collect sensitive religious beliefs or personal identifiers unless needed and consented.

1. Tell me about the last time you used a digital service for prayer, temple information, or a ritual.
2. What were you trying to accomplish? What prompted you to use the service?
3. Walk me through what you did, step by step. Where did you pause or need help?
4. How did you decide the service information and fulfilment were trustworthy?
5. What details did you have to enter? Which would you expect to be saved or checked?
6. What happened after booking or payment? What confirmation or follow-up did you expect?
7. Have you used the same service on more than one device or channel? What carried over?
8. Which parts felt useful or meaningful? Which felt confusing or intrusive?
9. If you could change one part of that journey, what would you change and why?
10. Is there anything I did not ask that would help me understand the experience?

Follow-up probes: “Can you give an example?”, “What happened next?”, “How did that affect your decision?” Avoid leading participants toward themes found in public reviews. Record consent and anonymize notes.
